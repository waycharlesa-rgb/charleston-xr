Downtown, at street level

Charleston XR

Walk or fly the peninsula from the Battery to Marion Square, in Google’s photographed 3D city. Clap to transmute the streets.

32.7760° N, 79.9300° W

Stored only on this device. Needs the Map Tiles API turned on in Google Cloud.

This browser can’t open a headset view. Open the page in Chrome on the Galaxy XR to step inside.

Controls

Hands

Left pinch
Hold to walk (or fly) where you’re looking
Both pinch
Hurry
Left pinch ×2
Two quick pinches: take off or land
Right pinch
Hold one second to travel to the next landmark
Turn
Turn your head and body

Alchemy

Clap
Charge your hands for five seconds
Touch ground
Spikes race away from you
Push
Stone bursts from what you’re looking at, ground or wall
Raise hands
A pillar lifts you 12 m up

Creations sink back into the street after a few minutes.

Vehicles

Menu
Turn your left palm up, then tap a row with your right index finger. With controllers, hold the left grip and point the right trigger at a row
Summon
A car or a carriage appears just ahead of you and you climb in. A sailboat takes you out to the nearest stretch of harbor
Steer
Hold your hands like a wheel and tilt them; right pinch to go, left pinch to brake or reverse
Sailing
The wind is from the southwest. Right pinch trims the sails in, left eases them. You can’t sail within about 40° of the wind
Get out
From the palm menu, or B / Y on controllers
